How it works

We apply economic pressure to mega conglomerates to influence positive change.

The rules

  • Large companies only, never a local business. The size floor is arithmetic on a company's own revenue, not somebody's judgment.
  • Criticise companies, never individual people. Named individuals are out of scope.
  • No hate, harassment, threats, or violence. Reports are actioned within 24 hours.
  • Not buying is the only tactic here. Loxley supports declining to buy something over how it is made or sold, and nothing else.
